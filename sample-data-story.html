What stands out

Steady gains,
different drivers.

Total revenue increased in every quarter, with Q4 delivering the strongest result. The North contributed the most revenue overall, while the West recorded the greatest percentage increase from the first to the final quarter.

For a leadership conversation, the next useful question is whether the West’s pace is sustainable—and what is enabling it.
