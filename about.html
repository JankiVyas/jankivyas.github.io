A balance of precision and feeling

Make complex
feel approachable.

For more than 12 years, I’ve worked in enterprise IT—across data engineering, Snowflake, AWS, and platform and solutions architecture. That experience taught me how organizations make decisions, how information moves, and why a clear explanation matters.

Alongside that work, I’ve built a fine-arts practice in charcoal, oil, and watercolor. It taught me to notice proportion, contrast, texture, and the small choices that give a composition its feeling.

Studio Clarity brings those perspectives together: rigorous about the message, thoughtful about the visual, and always focused on the people who need to understand it.
